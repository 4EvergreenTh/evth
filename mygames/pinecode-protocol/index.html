The Pinecode Protocol

A short RPG inspired by Earthbound, where you play as James, a teenager who loves old stuff. One day as he goes into the forest he discovers an old bunker filled with mysteries... James and his Aunt arrive to an old house. One day, as James was listening to some music on his Walkman when exploring around the house, he hears static in his player. Intrigued, he searches in the near-by forest for the mysterious sound origin. There he finds an old abandoned bunker whith strange retro-futuristic equipment.
